# Original Giessler angular-state source

Maximilian Giessler, *Kinematic data of a pendulum serving as an angular acceleration reference* (2025), Figshare article28271405 v1, DOI10.6084/m9.figshare.28271405.v1, CC BY4.0. Original article metadata and download receipt accompany the exact CSV bytes. The associated paper is DOI10.1038/s44172-025-00384-8; Table2 supplies rad-based kinematic units (attribution established during original acquisition, not newly researched here).

WORLDLAW selects all648 original timestamps0–3.235s: raw MOCAP angle, IMC(mean) rate, and IMC acceleration (header has trailing space). These are synchronized channels of one course, not independent repetitions. Model-prediction columns are excluded. Angle comes from a different instrument from rate/acceleration; alignment and sensor/channel accuracy are assumptions, not inferred calibration. JSON retains source labels/units; original CSVs retain CRLF and verified SHA256. The acceleration is an observed IMC channel, not a position derivative manufactured by WORLDLAW. No independent measured challenge is available. Fitted law forecasts are explicitly training reconstructions; the direct and integrated fits lose to the maintained position-only weak incumbent on this course.
