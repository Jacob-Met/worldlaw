A separate mark is evidence to inspect.

The interval uses only the reference mark’s declared radius. It does not include clock error, calibrated tracker error, physical accuracy or a probability. A high pixel-match score does not shrink that radius. A fixed pivot remains a declared location. Original measurements, revisions, gaps and angular evidence stay unchanged; these marks do not correct or train the tracker.

Reference coordinates, independence, frame times and PNG hashes are producer-declared and fallible. Attaching the original video verifies its identity and dimensions. The browser’s decoded image is shown at its own reported time; it is not authenticated against the producer’s PNG hash.
