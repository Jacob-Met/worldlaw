# Prospective physical acquisition study

The experiment compares selectors for recorded optical interventions on one Causal Chambers apparatus. The source is Gamella, Peters and Bühlmann, *Causal chambers as a real-world physical testbed for AI methodology*, DOI 10.1038/s42256-024-00964-x. The author experiment repository is pinned at 967d59a42956872f37463cc561ecf6e9ceab9364. The numerical data carry CC BY 4.0. The published archive lt_malus_v1.zip has MD5 cc49b95d85410e0b5ea3bcd1479428e3. The paper’s reference notebook at 52f418837cf56b474fc4d9704f0c41c3d1dbd7dd identifies ir_3 as the photometer and the relative-angle cosine-squared curve as the published physical reference.

The fixed library contains squared cosine of the polarizer angle difference, angle sum, first angle and second angle. Each template has one unknown constant offset and a positive gain. For each color its brightness-128 recording supplies a development slope and empirical per-reading tolerance. Readings are divided by that source slope times the target-to-development brightness ratio. Allowed target gains are 0.8 through 1.2 on this scale. The bound is an empirical development choice. A physical reading can falsify every template. Every selector and comparator uses the same four templates, gain range and per-color noise tolerance. No target measures are used to normalize ranges or to generate commands.

Target files comprise exactly the recorded 64 and 255 brightness runs for white, red, green and blue. The 8,000 angles come from the original seeded experiment generator and the motor’s development-verified 0.1125° setpoint grid. Each consecutive 50-angle block offers its first ten settings. The remaining 40 supply an auxiliary known-law reconstruction error, where available. The actual planner consumes only commanded angles represented as four fixed response basis terms. The existing Worldlaw exact affine-calibrated solver at 45db4cfa5694d5eaa5c5b2e807aebf416cdd3407 supplies the smallest certified subcollection up to six readings, equal cost for each setting, with one gain and offset constant within a course.

Include every one of 160 blocks. Success means that the supplied photometer readings uniquely retain the paper’s relative-angle law in the declared four-model family. Compare a regularized D-optimal selector with all four model features and an intercept, chronological first-k selection, and the exact average over all uniform k-subsets of ten settings. For a certified course all receive k equal to the certified reading count. On an uncertified course count the active method as abstaining and allow comparators the full six-reading ceiling. The primary gate requires a gain of at least 0.05 in the fraction uniquely retaining the published physical law over each comparator, a strictly positive 95% lower bound for the paired setting-level bootstrap (eight source recordings, 10,000 draws, seed 830) and zero wrong active identifications. Report abstentions, model incompatibility, actual acquired-read and comparator budgets, and the eight setting-specific rates. A model family containing the published answer cannot establish general law discovery. This is an offline experiment on recorded interventions. No live apparatus command is performed.
