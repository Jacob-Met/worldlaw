What this measurement can establish

Each segment uses linear torque interpolation between its declared endpoints. The saved angleAssumption explicitly treats angles as exact; angular uncertainty is unsupported. Torque radii are deterministic bounds, not confidence intervals. The integration allowance is an additional user-declared absolute work bound. It does not estimate missing curvature. No unobserved gaps or invented closing segments are accepted.

Even an exactly conservative nonlinear law can have nonzero polygonal work when outward and return sampling differ. The allowance must cover such interpolation error to test the continuous path. Try the conservative nonlinear control: torque = angle² gives −0.25 J for its polygon, despite zero exact continuous closed-path work.

Positive work means positive signed ∫τ dθ in the declared order and torque convention. A return to the same angle does not establish return of hidden, thermal or other state. Excluding zero may contradict a position-only conservative response only when the assumptions and allowance cover the continuous path; it does not identify dissipation, generated energy or a mechanism. Zero compatibility does not prove conservativity. The trace shows central readings; bounds appear in the reported interval.
