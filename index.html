    Scope

    WORLDLAW searches a finite library of scalar torque terms. The default mechanism and its observations are virtual. Model comparisons depend on the supplied evidence and noise assumption; model spread is not calibrated probability. This workbench does not infer mechanism topology or operate physical hardware.
