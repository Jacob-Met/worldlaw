# Keep competing explanations available for the next observation

A scalar library proposal now retains optional terms used by **every competitive
expanded model**, alongside the terms in the original applied library. The best
ranked model remains a provisional explanation. Its alternatives stay executable
after Apply, allowing the existing Studio planner to compare their predictions
at original offered inputs instead of silently losing a competing structure.

This uses the same complete eleven-term search, maximum model size, estimator,
noise and ambiguity threshold as the original library investigation. The
union adds no fit or search to the proposal. Its complete ordinary and robust
work reservations remain unchanged. Normal Apply refits the editable library
through the existing session; the existing planner, input-rank pilot and
measurement intake remain their maintained implementations.

Before Apply, expand **competitive expanded laws** to inspect every competing
equation, raw training error and score difference. The proposed checkboxes retain
their optional terms. A person can remove terms, but that removes those
explanations from the next fit and experiment comparison. Training rank and
ambiguity do not identify a unique physical mechanism.

## A source-grounded example

Import `examples/scalar-competing-terms.json`. This is an authored synthetic
course, not physical measurements. Its original 97 rows satisfy
`command = position²`, so a command-position term and a cubic-position term
make identical training predictions. With the explicitly chosen noise floor
0.0001 N·m, both four-term laws are competitive. Selecting just the first
law's additional term used to leave one model and zero planned disagreement.

Investigate the missing terms, inspect both equations, and Apply. The existing
planner now compares both models at the three originally permitted conditions.
The offer with command 0, position 1.3 rad and rate 0.2 rad/s separates them by
1.42805 N·m. The original-manifold offer remains uninformative. The maintained
rank pilot independently reports rank 8/9 on the retained library, with the
off-manifold offer reaching 9/9. These are conditional algebraic diagnostics;
the software does not authorize or perform hardware actuation.

A separately authored torque reading of −3.04805 N·m selects the cubic law;
−1.62 N·m selects the interaction law. Both use ordinary measured-outcome
intake. The cubic control improves its independent 61-row error from
1.8805328 N·m to numerical roundoff. Keeping only the first selected optional
term instead leaves residual mismatch and error 0.0276566 N·m after the same
reading. The initially correct interaction control has no substantive accuracy
gain to claim. All these are software controls, not calibrated physical results.

## Negative controls and boundaries

Offers confined to the original confounding manifold remain uninformative;
missing excitation is not a successful experiment. An incorrect entered torque
of 123 N·m leaves a training mismatch. Replacing every held-out torque with 999
N·m leaves the proposal and complete plan unchanged. Reopening the source-only
notebook restores the retained library and recomputes the same plan.

The finite competitive set depends on the declared noise and complexity
heuristic. It need not contain the true mechanism; preserving its alternatives
does not calibrate predictive uncertainty or prove that a rank-increasing
observation is accurate. Incorrect coordinate or noise declarations, omitted
physics and changed mechanisms can still defeat the whole library. A later
reading refits the admitted library through the normal revision mechanism;
this is not a promise to keep coefficients frozen after measurement.
