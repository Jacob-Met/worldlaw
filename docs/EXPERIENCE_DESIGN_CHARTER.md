# WORLDLAW — Experience design charter

> Mandatory product-specific UI direction. Read with `AGENTS.md`, `docs/PRODUCT_CANON.md` and the [HAMON experience standard](https://github.com/Jacob-Met/hamon/blob/docs/product-experience-standard-20261019/docs/hackathons/EXPERIENCE_DESIGN_STANDARD.md). This is a starting thesis, not a palette template.

## Experiential thesis
**An interactive scientific apparatus that lets people watch an AI propose, challenge, and revise physical laws in a world that moves.** It should feel like entering an extraordinary experiment laboratory, not configuring an ordinary analytics SaaS product.

## Signature canvas
Make the **actual simulated or observed mechanism** the hero. Put competing laws in direct visual confrontation: ghost trajectories, diverging predictions, force arrows, error envelopes, constraint overlays and experiments playing out in time. Ground every visual in the actual executable solver or measured source; never pass stylized animation off as validated physics. The scientific controls should feel like instruments associated with the apparatus, not generic card piles.

## Product-owned visual grammar
Explore instrument-grade spatial composition, purposeful depth, engineering illustration, high-quality procedural motion and rigorous labels. Build at least two materially different composition explorations rather than defaulting to cream/forest-green serif headlines + sidebar cards. The existing workbench may remain as an advanced science view, but it must not be the visual destination of the product. Favor clear, direct manipulation and vivid contrasting predictions over ornamental chrome.

## Demonstration moment
A user enters an unfamiliar mechanism, witnesses two candidate laws predict different motion, authorizes an informative experiment, watches that experiment run and sees the rival explanations react. A good first 20 seconds makes the distinction between reality, model forecast and uncertainty intuitive.

## Current implementation: improve, do not reset
WORLDLAW already has a runnable Discovery Studio, Trajectory Challenge and Motion Lab. Audit and preserve those real flows, datasets, saved notebooks and tested behavior. Upgrade their shared navigation and visual storytelling incrementally; connect the visual apparatus directly to working science. Do not replace the running product with a static concept or fake cinematic animation.

## Visual receiving gates
For meaningful interface changes, retain screenshots/video from the actual app at desktop and narrow sizes, keyboard/focus path, motion-reduction behavior and explicit source/forecast labels. Ask whether the central scientific action is visibly unique to WORLDLAW, not interchangeable with other project dashboards. Report the first major remaining design flaw and iterate.
