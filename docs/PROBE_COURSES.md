## Bounded work and source custody

At most eight states produce 28 two-state courses, each with at most six model comparisons. One shared 200,000 clip-work budget covers the entire request. Arithmetic or work exhaustion throws, so no partial ranking is returned. Original training admission is reused; challenge-bearing input is refused. Export retains only the schema, original training and acquisition settings. Reopen recomputes every candidate and selection; supplied derived results are ignored. No future outcomes enter fitting or selection.

Ranking maximizes separated pairs, then minimizes total declared cost among affordable courses. It does not compare a two-state course against a cheaper one-state acquisition automatically; the original chooser remains available. Pairwise separation need not provide a uniquely identified physical mechanism.
