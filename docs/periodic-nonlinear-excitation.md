# Integrating conservative fluxes with an exposed diffusion operator

On an even periodic ring, take alternating initial values −0.05, +0.05, spacing 0.2, diffusion 0.6 and zero command. Compare diffusion alone against diffusion plus 0.1 times the centered gradient of the squared field. Each cell has two identical neighbors, so the added flux is exactly zero. Both physical trajectories equal their initial alternating mode times exp(−4Dt/h²). The previous design paths used a Fourier step for one model and explicit RK4 for the other. On eight cells with four 0.1-second intervals they reported an RMS difference of 20,661.31.

The receiver checks whether the initial state and every commanded spatial pattern preserve a one- or two-cell repeating orbit. Within that orbit any defined centered gradient of a pointwise potential vanishes. To remove such a gradient, the receiver verifies the remaining expression is affine, has nonpositive state reaction and nonnegative diffusion, and bounds all arithmetic in the erased potential. The two-cell diffusion only mixes the two values. With a bounded drive, the maximum absolute state is bounded by its initial maximum plus the integrated bound on absolute forcing. A reciprocal or an overflowing potential must retain its original evaluation. Running the same reduced dynamics gives exactly zero measured separation, including at zero resolution.

For other periodic states, the receiver splits an exposed positive linear diffusion and any homogeneous affine terms from the remaining nonlinear source. Write x′=Lx+N(t,x). Each Lawson RK4 stage integrates its linear substep with the physical stencil's circular convolution kernel exp(τL). Nonlinear evaluations and the actual timed drive stay at their original state, cell and stage time. Both predictor fitting and the explicit public RK4 entry point retain their original behavior. The internal mixing-time rule budgets at least one stage interval per conservative spectral-rate scale |a|+|b|/h+4d/h². An insufficient step or convolution budget yields an explicit exclusion. The rule controls temporal resolution and provides no rigorous global truncation bound. Held-trajectory verification independently compares coarse and fine predictions.

With a nonuniform continuing sinusoidal drive, the same eight-cell nonlinear pair has RMS gaps 4.22e−7 and 1.67e−6 on two initial profiles. Both are unresolved at 1e−4. At 1e−6 only the lower spatial mode separates. The prior estimator reported 4.89 for that mode and excluded the Nyquist initial profile. Direct physical-stencil midpoint integration at 256 substeps per interval checks both gaps. Twelve changed nonlinear systems vary ring size, diffusion and transport direction under a chirp. All passed the 2e−7 trajectory and refinement gates, with maximum held difference 7.57e−8. Doubling the independent reference mesh moved its saved states by at most 5.70e−8. The original campaign's unseen-world calibration and evaluation remain separate.
