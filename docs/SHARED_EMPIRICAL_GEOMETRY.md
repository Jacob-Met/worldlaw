The original dataset documents five nominal lengths and a PASCO PS-3220 rotary sensor, but does not specify a pivot-to-centre-of-mass length definition, bob/rod/sensor inertia, or a mechanical end correction. See the author's [immutable dataset README](https://github.com/EnzeXu/Damped_Pendulum_Dataset/blob/cbf82673641ecd65e902ad5c38a048387649a2a0/README.md). Its linked OpenReview paper was blocked by browser verification during this inspection. No unobserved apparatus detail is assumed.

The optional consumer therefore fits an explicitly **empirical conditional relation**, not corrected physical geometry:

`angular restoring coefficient = −A / (nominal length + delta)`.

A and delta are model parameters. They are not identified gravity, effective length, inertia, or a measured end correction. Damping remains independent per original training course; receiving damping retains the original source-law coefficient under the existing unchanged-damping assumption.
