## Limits

Found endpoints are executable witnesses, not global extrema, confidence intervals or complete feasible bounds. Failure to find disagreement does not establish identification. The tolerance and coefficient bounds are caller assumptions, not calibrated uncertainty. Two solver resolutions do not prove convergence. The prospective release is declared, not newly acquired evidence. The operator preserves unknown physical inertia and torque scale. No new measured superiority is claimed; prior direct/integrated Giessler losses and bounded-start PASCO zero-width non-gain remain unchanged.

The current UI offers the analytic example and source-notebook intake; it does not automatically infer a coefficient box from the parent fit or optimize physical experiment design. Its two trajectories are actual solver outputs. All current changes remain unpublished until normal integration.
