## Scientific basis

For frequency f = 1/P, use z(f) = Σ w exp(i ω f), where ω = 2π(index − center). The common midpoint index origin preserves gain. Define L = Σ|wω| and M₂ = Σ|w|ω². In exact real arithmetic, a frequency cell with midpoint m and radius d has upper bound

`min(Σ|w|, |z(m)| + Ld, max(|z(m) − dz′(m)|, |z(m) + dz′(m)|) + M₂d²/2)`.

This follows from Lipschitz variation, Taylor's integral remainder, and convexity of the linearized complex norm. Both equal-width and largest-upper-cell refinement are retained. Sample witnesses include both endpoints. Adaptive stopping requires the requested gap, exhausted declared work, or no representable split. Uniform refinement spends its full declared budget. Every retained cell remains inspectable; no interval is silently dropped. Complete courses exceeding two million weighted evaluations across both strategies are refused before refinement. Existing finite-comparison admission remains required.

JavaScript sine, cosine, and arithmetic are not formally enclosed. Engineering allowances scale with weighted phase, derivative and remainder magnitudes, plus endpoint-frequency conversion scale. Witness lower estimates subtract their point allowance; upper estimates add a cell allowance. `engineering-gap-met` is not certified continuous immunity. `unresolved-budget` and `unresolved-resolution` preserve the witness, upper estimate, and every cell. No amplitude, calibration, frequency drift within a run, physical timing, or acquisition authority is inferred.
