# Creative Autonomy Charter — Expand the Fantasy, Not Just the Implementation

**User-directed correction (2026-10-18):** The existing 30 hackathon blueprints describe starting visions, not the limits of what autonomous developers may invent. **The agents are explicitly authorized and expected to improve the product concept itself, not only its architecture.** The user wants audacious, difficult, genuinely novel work, backed by complete functioning products.

## Precedence and freedom

1. **Protect the user's underlying dream**: the distinctive user benefit, identity of the concept, real-world rights/safety constraints, and any direct user-origin decisions. These are the anchor, not an arbitrary checklist chosen by an assistant.
2. **Treat assistant-created blueprints, demos, examples, algorithms, feature names, user journeys, UI assumptions, and manifest signature IDs as challengeable hypotheses.** They are an initial rich design exploration, not a prison. A better design may restructure, recombine, radically expand, or replace their mechanics.
3. **Creative invention is part of the job, not an extracurricular activity.** On every active product, find opportunities for a genuine leap: new user capabilities, unexpected interaction paradigms, novel AI/graphics/physics technology, new application domains, or stronger platform experiences. Act on high-potential ideas by prototyping and integrating them, not merely recording brainstorms.
4. **No automatic permission ceremony for creativity.** A real integration owner may run experiments, branch versions, implement exciting improvements, modify the design and accept new features based on evidence using existing estate authority and engineering review. Only direct changes to user-origin intent, consequential external actions requiring separate authorization, financial spend, or genuinely user-only credentials need appropriate escalation.
5. **No stealth downsizing.** When replacing a previously expected experience, map the original user benefit to the successor behavior and demonstrate that it is preserved or exceeded. Keep historical traceability. If a proposal weakens a user-origin commitment or diverges from the fundamental concept, do not silently relabel it as progress; keep a separate product direction or obtain user direction.
6. **Keep the whole application running.** Early vertical slices prove technical integration, never define the maximum scope. Incorporate breakthroughs into the actual consumer experience, test on real source material, and own the resulting video, deployment and authorized hackathon submission.

## How to discover something nobody originally specified

An autonomous product team should keep *both* the full source promise and an open invention frontier visible, using several independent complementary paths:

- **Divergent product invention:** Ask what would feel impossible or magical to the eventual user. Challenge the core flow, interface, setting, audience and expressiveness. Imagine entirely different, more memorable experiences compatible with the original promise.
- **Scientific/engineering invention:** Investigate adjacent research, unsolved physics/perception/inference problems, unconventional model combinations, radically different computational representations and principled experiments. A difficult possibility is a candidate experiment, not grounds for automatic exclusion.
- **Aesthetic invention:** Explore visual direction, interaction, sound, motion, character, dramaturgy and presentation with the same level of original thought as the technical architecture. The demo should make the invention palpable, not hide it behind developer dashboards.
- **Synthesis/integration:** Rapidly prototype strong ideas and compare them in the *running product*, without harming the source-grounded experience. Improve the best direction through user observation, meaningful qualitative judgments and technical measurements. Explore alternatives on independent branches.
- **Unexpected expansion:** Look for novel combinations between projects and shared primitives without turning every product into the same generic agent wrapper. If a feature is genuinely transformative, expand the vision and add its engineering graph and receiving tests.

The invention portfolio is not constrained to a fixed number of ideas, workers, experiments or iterations. It must not become an ideation-only treadmill. An invention that is judged worth keeping becomes concrete executable product work with an owning integrator.

## Proposed → prototyped → product-qualified (not an approval bottleneck)

A short **Creative Frontier** log may record, for each significant new direction:
- What previously impossible or surprising user experience does it enable?
- Why is it genuinely stronger than what the old spec described?
- Which user-origin promise remains, and which assistant assumptions are challenged?
- What hard technical breakthrough and experiment would distinguish its feasibility?
- What branch/code/demo proves an actual effect, and what limitations remain?
- If a legacy signature capability is implemented by different mechanics, how is the *same or greater benefit* independently demonstrated?
- Is this an exploration, accepted product commitment, or a different product concept altogether?

Agents may explore and implement before every question is answered, as long as provenance and rights are respected. These fields clarify decisions *after real work*, not gate imagination.

## Distinguish creative reimagination from abandonment

**Allowed autonomous example:** PHANTOM WINDOW invents a temporal scene-graph/neural cinematic director that allows viewers to meaningfully re-stage camera moves beyond the original static parallax specification. It changes the architecture and adds new directorial powers; the original "enter recorded footage and see genuinely different, temporally coherent rendered viewpoints" user benefit remains tested.

**Allowed autonomous example:** WORLDLAW invents an active robotic experiment pipeline and hybrid symbolic/learned law-discovery engine far beyond fitting preselected joint models. It reframes the interface and problem classes; the scientific discovery promise strengthens.

**Not an acceptable substitution:** REPLAY FORGE stops reconstructing source-grounded game events and shows a pretty tactical animation driven only by manually authored keyframes. That is a useful internal prototype, not the full originally intended product.

**Not an acceptable substitution:** FALSIFIER stops running real counterexample tests because agent-written reasoning "seems correct." A convincing writeup isn't the required independent proof.

## Machine-readable scope tracking (what automation can and cannot enforce)

- Preserve the source identity in `IDEA_PORTFOLIO_2026_SOURCE.md` and keep the original manifest capability IDs as **traceability anchors**.
- The original capability *implementation* is not immutable: report extra agent-invented capabilities with their own IDs, evidence and motivation. The checker can also accept a reimagined equivalent when it explicitly maps the old capability to a genuine independently demonstrated successor.
- The guard checks records, dependency coherence and honesty of **claims**. It cannot objectively determine whether a radical creative design is excellent or equivalent to the human product fantasy. That requires actual expert/user/product review and source-level judgment. Do not mistake JSON validity for originality.
- Never suppress new ideas merely because the existing checker has not yet learned their shape. Evolve the checker/contract while preserving the original trace.

## Mandatory continuous integration while inventing

The [Continuous Mainline Delivery Contract](CONTINUOUS_MAINLINE_DELIVERY.md) is equally authoritative: every active project must keep `main` a coherent runnable package. Merge proven incremental work promptly, keep major unfinished inventions behind optional feature flags and isolated routes, retain source and save-file compatibility, and preserve the default golden path. A long research epic is not a reason to delay harmless incremental merges, and passing CI alone is not evidence of the entire final vision. If the default experience breaks, revert or repair promptly. Agents invent the ultimate product **and** continuously improve the current usable one.

## Product-specific creative provocations (invitations, not limits)

- **REPLAY FORGE:** What if the viewer could reconstruct entire matches as persistent worlds, stage director-level film sequences and explore branching sport/team/game universes through a unified expressive conversation—not just revise one possession? Maintain evidence-versus-fiction distinctions.
- **PHANTOM WINDOW:** What if recorded footage becomes an explorable, editable temporal stage, with dynamic objects, new cinematography, lighting and viewpoint trajectories—while marking generated unseen details honestly?
- **CAPTIONVERSE:** What if spatial captions become a full multi-sensory language of storytelling, explaining events and conversational structure interactively without reducing accessibility?
- **DOMINO:** What if a home could automatically discover its own unintended causal behaviors and teach the resident through an interactive digital twin, then verify multiple repairs before any real actuation?
- **AFTERIMAGE:** What if it becomes a general-purpose abductive physical scientist that can propose sensors, move inspections, perform controlled experiments and radically improve understanding of events nobody witnessed?
- **WORLDLAW:** What if mechanisms are only the first domain of a system that invents scientific questions, builds instruments, experiments and new executable physical models?
- **SYNAPSE:** What if computation becomes a navigable manipulable world with actual temporal/causal depth and native multi-agent collaboration rather than a 3D monitoring dashboard?
- **KERNEL GARDEN:** What if it invents new computational algorithm families and hardware mappings rather than only tune parameters of human templates?
- **FALSIFIER:** What if it autonomously discovers missing specifications and builds the experiments needed to disprove even its own definition of correctness?

These are inspiration, **not** requirements to shoehorn every product into one idea or an excuse to delay building.

**Final operational imperative:** Invent more. Attempt harder breakthroughs. Iterate on the *product concept*. Integrate the inventions. Keep original human benefits intact. Deliver real exceptional products, record real results and complete authorized submissions.
