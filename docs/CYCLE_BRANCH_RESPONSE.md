# Frozen branch-response investigations

This additive Cycle Work consumer fits a declared three-family response library to complete original training cycles and challenges every unchanged family with separately supplied cycles. It addresses a gap in work-only comparison: a conservative response change can leave the closed polygon work unchanged.

## Models and observation meaning

The families are tau=a+b*theta; tau=a+b*theta+c*s; and tau=a+b*theta+s*(c+d*theta), where s is the explicitly declared +1 increasing-angle or -1 decreasing-angle branch. Coefficients remain in the original angular origin and N·m/rad conventions. The maintained column-normalized QR primitive fits central training readings only. Rank failures remain explicit.

A record retains its acquisition ID, entire normalized Cycle Work input and one branch label per original row. The path must contain exactly two contiguous monotone legs. Separate torque readings at the same angle mark the branch switch, and the final angle returns to the first. Missing gaps, unlabelled or hidden reversals and nonclosing records refuse. Equal-angle adjacent rows within a leg remain retained. These labels do not identify friction, physical hysteresis, velocity or time-dependent dynamics.

Exact declared angles are required for fitting and receiving. The separate angle-box work consumer remains available and is not a coefficient-uncertainty model for these nominal fits.

## Frozen receiving and diagnostics

The frozen request contains1–8 training cycles, at most8192 original rows, and explicit training/receiving RMS discrepancy gates with a required basis. Each cycle retains the maintained4096-row limit. Receiving independently admits1–8 cycles and at most8192 rows, requires its own acquisition identities and an explicit separately-acquired/not-used-for-fitting declaration, and binds to the exact normalized frozen request. Numerically identical observation/branch records cannot count as separate replication in this receiver, even if renamed; this refusal does not prove the records were copied.

All families, failures and per-cycle diagnostics are retained. Each trained family must pass every training cycle's gate to have compatible training status. A pooled score cannot hide another cycle's failure. Receiving only evaluates the unchanged functions at supplied coordinates; it does not choose a winner or use receiving values in fitting.

For each frozen prediction, a deterministic torque-radius box gives a lower and upper absolute residual, with an explicit engineering floating-point guard. RMS aggregation reports a lower/upper discrepancy range. A range wholly within its declared tolerance is compatible, wholly above is incompatible, otherwise unresolved. These residual bounds qualify one nominal fitted function; they do not propagate training uncertainty into coefficients or establish statistical confidence. Receiving angles outside the corresponding training branch's overall range remain scored but are labelled outside-training-range.

The existing ordered-polygon evaluator independently reports observed work and work of the model predictions at the same original angles and labels. If predicted torques exceed that evaluator's input limits, the response residual remains visible and predicted work is explicitly unavailable. Model work is conditional interpolation of predictions, not new acquisition or a continuous-path integral. Original torque radii and integration allowances remain attached to original work.

## Consumer composition

Two alternatives were considered: overlay all cycles and both branches on one torque-angle canvas, or synchronized increasing/decreasing branch panes. The split panes are implemented because repeated angles and branch jumps can obscure disagreements in a single overlay. Both panes share the chosen original cycle and model; the complete per-cycle/family table stays visible. Gold joins actual supplied readings, cyan dashed joins frozen predicted torques. A row inspector retains original acquisition identity, branch, source, torque radius and residual. No animation is substituted for measured evidence.

Training input edits invalidate the accepted result. Receiving edits hide old receiving diagnostics and disable export until the edited acquisition is admitted; training predictions remain available. Repeated failed receiving/export actions cannot clear that dirty state. Input-only notebooks retain original cycles and raw declarations. Reopening recomputes; saved derived claims are ignored. Async reads use a generation token and selected-File cleanup.

## Executed authored evidence

For tau=.3−1.2*theta+s*(.2+.1*theta), the full family recovers coefficients within1e−12 and transfers to separately sampled angles. Position-only and constant-branch-offset families retain their training failures. A separate receiving response with an additional conservative .4*theta term retains work near1.2J while contradicting the frozen full response with torque RMS above.4N·m. Position-only controls leave the nested families jointly compatible; an omitted cubic branch response fails every family. These are authored examples, not physical or blind qualification.

Nine focused tests cover analytic families, all candidate/per-cycle population, separate receiving noninterference, equal-work drift, omitted structure, branch identity, stale/copied acquisitions, out-of-range observations, rank failures, input-only replay and discrepancy declaration admission.
