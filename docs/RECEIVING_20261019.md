## Scientific observations

The default virtual instrument is separate from the fitter. Its law is `1.7 command − 0.25 rate − 2.4 sin(position) + 0.12` N·m plus small deterministic noise. The initial small-angle evidence makes spring and gravity difficult to distinguish. The fitter searches subsets of a declared seven-term library; it is structural selection within that library.

| Default received run | Before experiment | After experiment |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Fitting rows | 64 | 65 |
| Fixed held-out rows | 61 | 61 |
| Selected restoring family | Spring | Gravity |
| Held-out RMSE, N·m | 1.645293629135742 | 0.0009572816573700754 |

The selected candidate position was +2.4 rad. The proposed-input disagreement and score are heuristics, not posterior probabilities. Held-out outcomes do not enter fitting, candidate ranking, or acquisition. No imported source is silently given access to the virtual measurement oracle.

`node experiments/active-discovery.mjs` retains results for 40 varied synthetic cases in `evidence/active-discovery-results.json`. Active measurements beat passive comparison in 20/20 gravity cases and 17/20 spring cases. The restoring family was correct in 40/40; the exact full term set was recovered in 19/20 cases for each family. A small bias below the assumed noise remained ambiguous. Omitted cubic terms and parameter drift remain counterexamples rather than being removed from the scope statement. See `SCIENTIFIC_CORE.md` for construction, score, diagnostics, and limits.

The trajectory adapter accepts time, calibrated angle, command, declared inertia, and position noise. It derives local polynomial estimates and records source hashes, assumptions, and window diagnostics. Its split excludes crossing derivative windows to prevent fit/held-out reuse of raw observations. Synthetic harmonic receiving demonstrates conversion into the actual model consumer; correlated derivative error and smoothing bias remain limitations. This does not establish independently acquired physical evidence.
