# Compare coefficients fitted to complete training motion

The predictive-selection apparatus can now explicitly compare a local coefficient refinement against its unchanged selected law. The original selector chooses a finite structure using omitted-training-recording prediction, then estimates its final coefficients from pooled weak equations. The optional comparison instead adjusts those coefficients against complete forward trajectories of the original training recordings. It does not add terms, change the selected structure, estimate initial conditions, reset trajectories, or use challenge observations during fitting.

The original weak-fit leader and predictive-selected model remain present. A magenta ghost trajectory shows the additional refined prediction against the same separately supplied observations. Improvement on training is described only as training improvement; it never replaces the incumbent or establishes physical validity. Every attempted coefficient vector, per-run execution status, numerical screen and training error remains inspectable.

## Method and work

The browser reuses `rolloutAngularWithResolution` and `reservePredictionWork`. For each coefficient in source order, a deterministic coordinate search tries both signs of its current step, accepts only a strict complete-training RMSE reduction, and halves all steps after a sweep without improvement. The initial step is the larger of 10% of that coefficient's magnitude and 0.01 in its own units. These are explicitly retained numerical heuristics, not uncertainty estimates or coordinate-invariant quantities. The default runs 20 sweeps, with at most 30 available, and does not claim a local-stationarity or global-optimality certificate.

The complete worst-case evaluation count is reserved before any trial: `1 + 2 × coefficients × sweeps`, including the original coefficients. Every trial executes both maintained step resolutions at every original training timestamp. The default coarse step is 0.02 seconds and the required two-step position difference is at most 0.001 rad. A failed or unresolved trial cannot become the refined model. An unresolved baseline stops the optional investigation. The full reservation must fit within 2 million integration steps; no source rows, model terms or trials are silently removed to fit that budget. Rows from intermediate rollouts are released after their summaries are retained.

The objective pools original noninitial row errors by count. Sampling density therefore affects its weighting. Fixed initial position and supplied rate can carry errors into every prediction; this search can compensate through coefficients. It applies no coefficient-sign or passivity constraint and identifies neither inertia nor absolute torque. A constant zero-term law cannot invent a coefficient improvement.

The independent challenge executes only after the optional training search finishes. A failed additional challenge reservation withholds the refined prediction while preserving the original comparison. Failure never substitutes another coefficient vector chosen from challenge error.

## Existing components inspected

WORLDLAW's `science/robust-fit.mjs` solves a fixed-design convex soft-L1 regression through weighted QR. `science/shared-training-search.mjs` searches linear source-error envelopes and checks witnesses. Neither supplies a nonlinear free-trajectory coefficient optimizer. They remain unchanged.

The maintained TOGISHI `engine/adapters/worldlaw.py` and `worldlaw_angular.py` interfaces inspect a fixed model over offered integration steps, comparing it to a frozen finer reference; they deliberately keep model coefficients outside the optimizer genome. `mechanism_strategy.py` compares offered tracker implementations and search radii through a Python-to-Node evaluator. Native issues `hamon-memory-research#8` and `#531` retain their existing ownership and holds. Adapting those interfaces would add an external runtime and a new evaluator without providing this small in-browser coefficient search. This increment therefore keeps its bounded local search beside the actual consumer while reusing the maintained executable solver and work accounting. Derivative-free search also avoids assuming smooth gradients through the Coulomb sign discontinuity.

## Evidence and retained failure

An independent analytic damped oscillator, contaminated only in training positions, supplies the positive control. Its challenge uses a different release and exact analytic motion. The maintained native course checks improvement against the unchanged incumbent, preservation of source rows and physical-scale uncertainty, and invariance of fitted coefficients and prediction curves under later challenge perturbation.

The first 30-sweep exploratory prototype (before production per-trial numerical screening) improved that control's challenge RMSE from 0.000190875 to 0.0000004136 rad. In the already reused PASCO course, the same experiment reduced training errors from approximately 0.181/0.180 to 0.041/0.052 rad and omitted-training-run errors from 0.155/0.097 to 0.059/0.063 rad, yet worsened the separate recording from 0.08951 to 0.18687 rad. Thus even the improved training-validation result did not predict the direction of transfer. This measured loss remains evidence against promoting the refinement as a generally superior physical estimator. Production defaults and their actual receiving results are recorded separately; prototype numbers are not presented as production output.

## Replay and interaction

Ordinary comparisons still export the original v1 input notebook. An explicit refinement exports v2 containing only the original bundle, selection options and refinement options. Reopening recomputes all coefficients, trials and challenges; imported derived results are never trusted. Old v1 notebooks retain their old behavior. Editing the opt-in invalidates saving, and replacing source recordings resets it.

A separate optimization page would hide the baseline and make it easy to mistake a lower training loss for a better law. The chosen inline comparison preserves the existing moving-law confrontation: original predictions and the optional magenta curve share the same observed motion. A collapsed trial inspection exposes the search without displacing the trajectory. Desktop, narrow 390 px, keyboard opt-in, real download/reopen, refusal and measured-loss paths are exercised by `scripts/forward-refinement-browser.mjs` with mandatory observed Chromium sandbox admission.
