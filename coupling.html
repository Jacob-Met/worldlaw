    Acquisition and measured accuracy

    The two clocks were frozen before scoring 50 DFKI hardware runs. The separation clock identified the port in 40 runs at a median of 30.3 ms. The fixed clock identified all 50 at a median of 101.9 ms. All ten early errors came from elbow-driven MC-PILCO runs.

    The replay uses the publisher’s common rigid-body parameters. It sums both controller channels before inference. Each clock uses the same initial encoder state and one subsequent snapshot. The recorded feedback controller generated the command history.

    A local response hypothesis.

    This lab compares three affine two-coordinate laws under a supplied quasi-static assumption. Both coordinates must use radians and conjugate torque signs in N·m. Hidden rates, changing loads, drift and nonlinear behavior can invalidate every offered explanation. Neither a fitted matrix nor the virtual instrument establishes mechanism topology, causal direction, physical safety or scientific validity.
