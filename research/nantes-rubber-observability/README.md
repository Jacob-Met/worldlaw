# What four heterogeneous rubber tests reveal about a constitutive ambiguity

Two incompressible isotropic stored energies agree in all uniaxial and equibiaxial states and differ by **20/143 (13.986%)** in nominal planar-extension stress at stretch 7/4. In the four measured Nantes SBR membrane designs, the alternative changes stress in the interior. The weak-work difference obtained using a virtual displacement supported at the lower grip nodes is zero in all 2,999 frames. Extending the same boundary displacement harmonically through the membrane yields a small nonzero internal-work difference. These observations quantify what different parts of these measured displacement fields can distinguish. No experimental force or stress value entered the study.

## Exact construction

For positive shear modulus μ, let v be the decreasing vector of singular values of the deformation gradient, s=(2,1,1/2), and restrict physical deformations to det F=1. In units of μ, define

- W₀=‖F‖²/2.
- L=s·v−‖s‖²/2+0.045.
- W₁=0.2W₀+0.8[W₀+h(L−W₀)], where h(d) is 0 for d≤−ε, d for d≥ε, and (d+ε)²/(4ε) in between; ε=0.005.

Up to its additive constant, L is the sum of the largest-one, half the largest-two sum and half the largest-three sum of the singular values. Each term is convex in F. The smoothed maximum is convex and nondecreasing in its two arguments (the derivative of h is in [0,1]). W₁ is globally at least 0.2μ-strongly convex in F before the determinant restriction and is therefore polyconvex. Both energies are objective and isotropic. Constants can set their common reference energy to zero. Incompressibility supplies the reference pressure.

The distance from s to either repeated-stretch plane is at least √(1/8). Hence W₀−L≥7/400>ε near every uniaxial or equibiaxial state, including repeated-stretch nonsmoothness of L. The smoothed energies and their gradients coincide there. At F=diag(7/4,1,4/7), L−W₀=439/39200>ε. Eliminating the thickness stress with the incompressible pressure gives nominal axial stresses 2145/1372 and 2445/1372 in units of μ. Their difference is 75/343, or 20/143 of the baseline. This result concerns this stable class and the specified loading paths. It does not identify a material from data.

## Measured loading paths

The source is Costecalde's four experimental six-hole, carbon-black-filled SBR membrane datasets, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.10033038](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10033038), and the accompanying zenoDoc.pdf (SHA-256 eb8e88e4a6ba44dc809d821366a3f1a441d7ce38e516fc8ef9b053d796a00422). The synthetic Ogden dogbone was excluded. Each experimental MATLAB object was checked against the author's MD5 and byte count before decoding. Both public filename extensions have MATLAB 7.3 container signatures. The analysis uses the four .mat files. Only U, X, Connectivity, Dirichlet indices, Force_sets indices and H (Hencky strain) are dereferenced. F appears in three experiment structs and is absent from ANR_1-2_v0066. The first case’s separately authenticated .ascii container also omits F and has 499 records. Its file body after the 512-byte header differs from the .mat body, so equal byte sizes do not establish interchangeable payloads. Numerical force entries in the other three structs remain unopened throughout. The sums below count correlated element-frames in four loading paths.

For each triangular element the code reconstructs the in-plane deformation gradient from reference edges and displaced edges. The thickness stretch follows from det F=1. All gradients have positive in-plane determinant (minimum 0.85895). The maximum difference between principal logarithmic strains from this reconstruction and those of stored H is 7.35×10⁻¹⁴. Each run's reference coordinates, connectivity and indexed force nodes stay fixed across its frames. Every Force_sets index roster equals the named lower boundary, bnd_down. The numbers of such nodes are 57, 53, 58 and 58.

For each Cartesian direction, the lower-grip selector is one on the measured boundary nodes and zero at every other node. A second operator fixes the same values on the complete exterior and hole boundaries, then solves the scalar finite-element Laplace problem on the interior. For each field φ the reported vector is the reference-area integral of P∇φ per unit initial thickness and shear modulus. The calculation uses source coordinate lengths with no conversion to newtons. RMS ratios use Euclidean norms over all components and all frames within a run.

All four grip selectors give exactly zero change. An independent raw-file reconstruction using the closed-form singular values of a 2×2 map found zero activated observations among 334,889 triangle-frames incident to any measured grip node. The closest came at ANR_3-1_v00066, frame 855, triangle 3390 (both zero-based), with squared stretch-space distance 0.1186753 against the strict 0.10 activation cutoff. Thus the zero selector difference has a geometric cause and does not rely on cancellation. Harmonic extensions change on 403, 366, 792 and 757 frames. The largest raw change occurs at zero-based frame 989 in ANR_3-1_v00066, with baseline vector (−125.763, −1005.442) and difference (+1.218, −2.101) in source-scaled units. The relative separation is small for this particular internal-work functional. These conditional calculations use the same measured displacements and material scale for W₀ and W₁, with no fit.

Two admissible virtual fields with the same boundary trace differ by a zero-trace test field. Variation of predicted internal work between the two is therefore an interior weak-equilibrium residual. An interpretation as the same experimental grip reaction also requires equilibrium, source boundary conditions, applicable body/inertial loads and pressure or plane-stress assumptions. The two functionals cannot both identify a measured reaction from the kinematic calculation alone. The interior fields do carry information about the two stress laws.

## Boundary of the experimental claim

The archive documents camera rates, exposure and within-struct frame indexing. The four filenames and mesh sizes bind each DIC design to its published case. The first two reference meshes have x-spans of 1034.77 and 87.1421 in their respective source coordinates. Cross-run dimensional work therefore requires an explicit per-file conversion. The checked archive documentation does not bind an initial thickness and length calibration for each file, the polarity/coordinate transform between camera components and the load cell, or measured force acquisition, filtering and synchronization to the DIC frames. Equal MATLAB struct indices alone do not establish all those conditions. The authors' related 2024 article (DOI [10.1016/j.polymertesting.2024.108521](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.polymertesting.2024.108521)) describes a different, aged-polychloroprene experiment; its documented synchronization cannot be transferred to the four SBR records. Access to the linked full thesis through the public document endpoint timed out during this study. These are source-scoped limits and do not establish that the original laboratory lacked the calibrations.

The experimental force-comparison gate remained closed. For a subsequent force comparison, admissible same-information reference models include an incompressible neo-Hookean scale fit, a nonnegative-coefficient Mooney–Rivlin fit (convex in F and its cofactor), and a predeclared nonnegative sum of convex singular-value powers. Each would receive the same training-run reactions, measured displacement inputs and predeclared test-run split. The authors’ data-driven reconstruction uses equilibrium and load information; fitting or reconstructing with a test run’s own forces would change that information budget. These references were not fitted or ranked in this design-only study. There is no measured force-error score, constitutive-model winner, parameter fit, or noise-floor inference. A fair response comparison would need the physical channel/clock metadata and the same kinematic information and training force budget for established fitted competitors. The exact theoretical witness and design projection stand independently of any future comparison.

## Reproduction

With Python 3.12 and NumPy, run `python replay.py` in this directory to check the rational witness, independent finite-difference gradient controls and saved counts. Install h5py and SciPy for the full raw-source replay. `python replay.py --download` retrieves the four official MATLAB objects, authenticates each, rebuilds both projection operators and independently recounts the lower-grip incident elements. `--source-dir DIRECTORY` uses pre-downloaded files. With the first alternate file in hand, add `--alternate ANR_1-2_v0066_experimental_data.ascii` to authenticate that version and inspect its field roster without dereferencing force data. Source data license and provenance are given in the Zenodo record. The stored results include the worst raw projection components and per-run counts.
