# Pre-outcome admission for the processed-power study

A registered raw AFI likelihood has already closed unscored in post 180653. This is a separate question about observable reconstructed phantom-image power.

## Scientific target and split

Forecast the mean of individual squared magnitude samples at nominal angles 1, 1.7627 and 3.1072 degrees from images at 5.4772, 9.6549, 17.019 and 30 degrees and the released processed B1 field. The quantity is squared magnitude after image reconstruction. Every arm is evaluated in the same dimensionless power units and squared-error loss. No square root of an expected power is used as an expectation of magnitude. All four scanners measured one traveling phantom. Scanner identity, VFA MID and repeat do not provide independent phantoms. Commercial_1 and Prototype_1 are six development runs; Commercial_2 and Prototype_2 are six external runs. Inclusion is every source-labeled phantom VFA run on each scanner. The two files in HumanSubject and all raw nuisance outcomes remain excluded. Neither standalone IRSE nor individual NMR references are required or opened for a processed-power target.

## Input-only source test

Before any lower-angle, stored fit, mask, or reference sample can be used, select only the four higher image volumes and the independently acquired processed B1 input from each development MAT. Match archive size, SHA and array shape. Compressed MAT containers may be inflated as bytes; the low three image channel byte spans are never converted into numerical values by the trusted input projector. The four high-angle images supply the signal measurements for every forecast. Source vial masks use sums across all angles and are forbidden for model fitting and primary endpoint membership.

The per-run normalization is the 95th percentile of the 30-degree image in the central box x=16:112, y=16:112, z=8:48 (zero-based half-open indices). An absent, nonfinite or nonpositive scale fails the whole admission. All intensities are divided by that scale before squaring. Positive multiplication of the input stack by c multiplies its normalization by c and leaves all normalized input values, power estimates, masks, forecasts and losses unchanged when targets use the same scale. This cancels the source's common all-echo scalar gain. It does not undo adaptive coil combination.

Use four 8×8 outer corner boxes at x,y in {0:8,120:128}. Use the separate face-centered 8×8 boxes at (0:8,60:68), (120:128,60:68), (60:68,0:8), (60:68,120:128) as a spatial validation of the corner-based noise term. Only scanner slices 8:48 enter. For each angle and each set, compute the mean square of all its samples. The candidate floor is the median of the four high-angle corner mean powers. All six development runs must return finite strictly positive powers, at least 95% nonzero corner samples, worst high-angle maximum/minimum corner mean-power ratio at most 1.50, and every high-angle validation-box mean power within [0.67,1.50] times that run's frozen floor. Failure closes transfer at the source/input gate without any low-angle or held-scanner image values. These are registered engineering comparability checks, not calibrated noise bounds.

Input-only foreground is the central box above intersected with a 30-degree intensity over 10% of the run scale and B1 in [0.5,1.5]. Empty support fails and excluded fraction is reported. This measured high-only selection does not represent all vial interiors or a known salt composition. Compute the fraction of the predicted 1° power explained by the calibration floor using the high-four source formula at each eligible voxel, with a nonphysical fit recorded separately. Requiring the median predicted fraction to exceed 1% on every development run prevents an unsupported small effect from consuming external endpoints. Every gate on all six development runs must pass; no case or air box may be removed afterward.

The primary comparison, if admitted, will include freshly source-matched high-four qMRLab SPGR for every run, same qMRLab plus the fixed output power term, conventional high-four nonlinear amplitude fitting, and identical nonlinear power fitting with and without that fixed term. A sufficiently strong same-information development-trained reference will be frozen before opening any external endpoints. Archived MID574 output fitted all seven and is excluded from forecast construction. Raw receiver covariance, realized coil weights and a build pin for each scanner are unavailable. Any eventual benefit would be conditional processed-image transfer.
