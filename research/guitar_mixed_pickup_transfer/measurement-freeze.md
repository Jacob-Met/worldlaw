The primary measurement uses stable_mode_features.py. Choose the highest-energy passing frame, earliest if tied. Use one joint frame, common frequency bins and intact polarity for simultaneous single channels. Score equal-mode RMSE in dB, then equal-take means; report each string, switch and position as well as raw worst same-mode observations.
