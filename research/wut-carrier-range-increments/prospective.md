# Measured E5b phase increment, prospective course

The authenticated two-receiver archives contain 48 RINEX 3.04 observation files. WUT0 serial3069597 and WUT1 serial4100503 report firmware5.5.0 on the same LEIAT504GG antenna with a GPS Source S14 splitter and a 30m antenna cable. Both declare GPS time, 1 s and the same complete pilot phase codes L1C, L5Q and L7Q with explicitly zero phase shifts. For this course use only these three codes, with E5b (L7Q) excluded from both receiver inputs. E5a and E5b are transmitted as components of the same AltBOC signal. Per-channel tracking noise independence is an assumption in one arm. Author methods include the registered 2026 E5a/E5b/E6 meta-signal pseudorange and the 2024 quad-frequency HMW work cited in the released README. Neither is a held-E5b carrier-increment predictor with the two-donor information here.

## Eight fixed procedures

Let a0,a1 be E5a range increments and d0,d1 the E1-minus-E5a differences. Set m=(d0+d1)/2, d=(d0−d1)/2. Write s=+1 for WUT0 and −1 for WUT1. The eight arms are own nearest E5a, own first-order (a_r+q d_r), average slope (a_r+q m), primary equal-noise receiver correction (a_r+q m+s d/2), train-only differential coefficient (a_r+q m+s w d; least squares on the receiver differences), train-only symmetric two-slope least squares (a_r+v m+s w d), separate full affine least squares, and separate full affine Huber regression. Use q=1381457/12173057. The two full regressors access exactly the four donor increments and an intercept, using the train-standardized coordinates a-mean, a-half-difference, m, d. Fit a residual relative to each receiver's own E5a. Huber IRLS uses 20 updates, starting at ordinary least squares, with a fixed per-receiver training threshold max(0.001m,1.345×1.4826×MAD of initial residuals). Ordinary solves use numpy.linalg.lstsq with rcond=1e-12. Retain training rank, coefficients, thresholds, and common/disagreement residuals.

The primary estimator and own-first-order give identical receiver-pair mean predictions; any squared-error difference between those two comes solely from receiver disagreement. The equal-noise choice corresponds to a 1/2 weight on each donor band for the receiver difference. For one observing site, repeated samples do not supply an uncertainty interval for other sites or satellite constellations.

## Decision

For each of six complete evaluation hours compute RMS error in metres over both receivers' paired scoreable rows. Then compute equal-hour mean and maximum hour RMS. The primary equal-noise arm must strictly improve both summaries over (i) own first-order and (ii) the strongest of the seven other arms (best separately per summary). There are four explicit criteria, with the two strongest-arm criteria logically including the first two. All six hours need at least one numeric pair; preserve an empty hour as a course qualification failure. Report clean-target diagnostics, the whole roster, each comparator and each losing margin. A failed development gate keeps final hours 18–23 sealed.
