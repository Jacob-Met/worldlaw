# When can measuring force add information about an early planar push?

This is a conditional source result. It makes no contact classification on an experimental push. Let the physical contact point relative to the object center be r, let J rotate a two-vector counterclockwise by 90 degrees, and pick a fixed positive reference length ell. For generalized velocity z=(vx,vy,ell*omega) and wrench w=(Fx,Fy,M/ell), their dot product equals mechanical power. The 2×3 matrix B=[I,Jr/ell] maps z to the material velocity at the contact.

Suppose a single compressive force f on the object acts at r without a separate contact couple. Its generalized wrench is B-transpose f. Assume quasistatic sliding on a support described by a constant symmetric positive-definite ellipsoidal mobility H and positive scale k, so z=k H B-transpose f. Complete sticking against the pusher gives Bz=u, the velocity of the pusher material point touching the object, including any spin term. Then

    z = H B-transpose (B H B-transpose) inverse u.

This follows because B has two independent rows, so B H B-transpose is positive definite for every r. Thus known geometry, u and the *same fixed H* suffice. The virtual force (B H B-transpose) inverse u gives the theoretical force direction when nonzero. Unknown scale prevents force magnitude identification. Multiplying H by a common positive factor leaves z unchanged. Positive definiteness alone does not ensure unilateral compression, compliance with the friction cone, or that contact persists.

For the isotropic translational ellipse with torsional radius c, in physical units j=Jr, omega=(j dot u)/(c²+r dot r), and v=u-j*omega. The measured force must be positively collinear with v. Writing A=u cross f and D=(j dot u)(j cross f)−(r dot r)A yields the constraint A*c²=D. A nonzero A identifies a finite candidate c² under the model only if D/A is positive and well-conditioned. A=0 with nonzero D falsifies the collinearity premise; A=D=0 only removes that constraint. A central normal push gives A=D=0 for every positive c. Forty or a hundred such repetitions would not fix the missing degree of freedom.

If the contact is sliding, only normal velocity compatibility remains. A measured compressive force direction can then select one candidate twist among different admissible tangential motions. Selective improvement could also reflect noisy kinematics, an incorrect shared H, changing surface conditions or a violation of the quasistatic assumption. Thus an incremental forecast gain would not identify a slipping contact. The geometry-plus-contact-velocity model above is a stronger source-matched baseline than constant-velocity extrapolation alone.

The scripts conditional_contact.py, sticking_control.py and sticking_operator.py use synthetic inputs only. The last checks 100 exact-rational configurations including anisotropy, force/rotation coupling, scale invariance and centered failure of identification. A real comparison must freeze a training-only estimate or authored choice for H and c, use the same measured input budget across roles, and separately count refusal conditions. No physical response was used to choose these controls.

## The unresolved resistance component on one face

Let A=H inverse and q=(−Jr/ell,1), so Bq=0. With a point force and no free couple, q-transpose A z=0 under the ellipsoid law. These are linear equations in the six entries of symmetric A and do not require full sticking. The rank of their design limits which resistance components can be recovered from measured poses. On the square face at x=−ell in object coordinates, q=(r_y/ell,1,1). For every contact on that face q is orthogonal to e=(0,1,−1), so the family A+eta e e-transpose with eta>0 consists of positive resistances having identical point-force constraints there. The result requires *actual contact on that face*, not just its nominal start. No conclusion is drawn about corner or other-face contact.

This ambiguity preserves predicted motion under same-face sticking. Bz=u leaves a one-dimensional freedom along q. The remaining scalar equation q-transpose A z=0 removes that freedom uniquely because q-transpose A q is positive. Changing A by eta e e-transpose leaves the equation unchanged. On another face q need not be orthogonal to e. Force measurements can also distinguish the family: A z and the changed resistance times z may map to different contact force directions, even though the same-face sticking velocities are identical. The resistance could therefore be better identified without improving those conditional kinematic forecasts.

one_face_observability.py builds an exact rational example using both positive resistances. All 33 combinations of 11 face locations and three force directions return exactly the same scaled sticking twist, and the kinematic design has rank four. Both virtual force directions are compressive in each case, yet all 33 pairs differ in direction. The different-face example gives different twists at the same true imposed contact velocity. All synthetic forces satisfy absolute tangential/normal ratio at most 733/1344, about 0.545. No experimental friction coefficient is assumed. These finite checks illustrate the analytic result, not a measurement of this apparatus.
