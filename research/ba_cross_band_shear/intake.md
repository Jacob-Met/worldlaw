The prospective question is whether a common frequency-dependent change in bulk and shear moduli transfers from the 1–100 Hz experiment to an ultrasonic shear speed conditioned on measured ultrasonic compressional speed. For isotropic elasticity and fixed density, x=Vp² equals (K+4G/3)/rho and y=Vs² equals G/rho. When both moduli share a scalar relaxation profile with nonnegative amplitudes, dy/dx lies in [0,3/4]. This additional model assumption requires frequency and pressure matching, and low-loss velocities or an independently qualified phase-to-storage correction. General passivity alone does not supply it. Distinct P/S pulse frequencies, unknown relative instrument calibration, density/state changes, anisotropy and multiple relaxations can break the relation. A pressure series on one plug is not a set of independent specimens.
