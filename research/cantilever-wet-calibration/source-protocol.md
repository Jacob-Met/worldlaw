# Dry and wet calibration of two immersed cantilever modes

Primary source: Payam, Trewby and Voïtchovsky, Analyst 142, 1492–1498, DOI 10.1039/C6AN02674E. This records the source-qualification contract before any experimental resonance ordinate or density/viscosity reconstruction is opened.

## Source rights

Inventory both main XML tables, the complete supplementary archive member roster, SI equations and captions. Inspect the original equation 2 graphic, methods, plot axes and legend. The only experimental Fig. 2 raster projections released at qualification are the outer plot/axis locations, marginal horizontal color counts and the surrounding margins with all four experimental panels masked. Rows/chemical identities must be assigned independently of frequency values. Figure 3's fitted vs measured pair and the PEO estimates are outside calibration. Six standards are isopropanol, acetone, butanol, decane, bromoform and hexanol, with ultrapure water as reference. Pure liquids and PEO belong to separate instruments.

## Air plus water

The original two-mode estimator requires both measured air angular frequencies and both measured water angular frequencies for the same cantilever. Check for all eight air frequencies assigned to four probes, or a source-justified interval for each. Nominal geometry, an ideal dry-mode ratio, a fit using the other known liquids, or digitization of a derived fluid property carries different information. When the required air values are unavailable, report this estimator unscored. The finite list and its data-access routes bound the inventory.

## Effective source equation and exact controls

Equation 2 uses angular frequency in radians/s. Absorb the geometry and source constants into two nonnegative effective parameters per probe. Put t = sqrt(omega_wet), xi = sqrt(rho eta); the polynomial for each probe, fluid and mode is (1+a rho)t^4 + b xi t^3 - F^2. With positive density, nonnegative effective parameters and F>0 there is exactly one positive root.

Use exact fractions to verify all coefficient identities under rho' = s rho+(1-s)rho_w, xi'=xi, d=s-a(1-s)rho_w, a'=a/d, lambda=s/d, b'=lambda b, F'^2=lambda F^2. Use positive s,d,rho', multiple unequal probes and modes, and signed perturbations of s around 1. Confirm all water properties and every within-probe dry modal ratio. Test independent second known density and a known absolute dry frequency as countercontrols that break this nontrivial family. This is a water-only, probe-specific effective class, under which absolute nonwater density and viscosity can vary at precisely the same wet frequencies. The published pure-liquid literature values provide independent standards if admitted.

Test two known wet standards by eliminating F^2 modewise. Use a positive, consistent synthetic example with a nonsingular system and another with exactly proportional wet frequencies and singular calibration. Treat rank, positive inferred coefficients and uncertainty in digitized frequencies separately. Equations alone do not establish actual instrument or aliquot uncertainty. No synthetic test counts as a measured predictive score.

## Figure-qualification boundary

A separate figure-endpoint experiment requires unique chemical/geometry/mode assignment, a fixed complete roster, resolvable marks/error bars, and a matched wet-calibration comparator. Count duplicate viscosity and density panels as views of the same ordinates. A graphical point is an estimate of a printed frequency. Shared sample names do not establish simultaneous calibration or independent replicates. Preserve unresolved overlap and omit no failed case to obtain a score.
