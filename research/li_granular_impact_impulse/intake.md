# Basal force fluctuations of a polydisperse gravity-driven granular flow

Li, Wang, Cheng and colleagues used angular quartz in five sieve bins, grain-size distributions with D=1.5–3.5, masses of 4, 8 and 12 kg and a 40° flume. The methods name three to four repeated releases per condition, a flush 4×4 cm force plate, static load calibration, and two collocated 1 kHz cameras (PIV profiles in the deposit have coarser time sampling). Determine the actual independently recorded experiment IDs, trace availability, alignment, units, baseline procedures, plate response, and common physical time windows before measured forces.

For marked independent impacts, if F(t)=sum J_i k(t-t_i) for a stationary Poisson arrival rate and a common unit-integral impulse response, E F=lambda E J and the two-sided integrated covariance is lambda E[J²]. Dividing these gives the impulse-size-weighted effective impulse E[J²]/E[J]. This is a conditional shot-noise model. An enduring contact contribution, correlated impacts, varying envelope or uncertain plate gain alters the relation. A finite record, bandwidth and nonstationary event onset do not measure the zero-frequency limit directly. Compare the full finite-window source-supported covariance or variance transfer against matched empirical frequency/condition controls and the authors’ Bagnold-inspired inertial-stress relation. Do not identify individual impacts or uniquely infer a grain size from one trace.

A full overlapping 20-sample moving mean on every native time step may retain enough equations to recover high-frequency samples when combined with boundary conditions. No whole sliding-mean trace may enter as an independent held-covariance predictor. If conditioning on a mean, freeze only a low-dimensional summary or strictly disjoint block integrals, disclose the measured held information, and compare every role with exactly the same summary. Alternatively use independently acquired PIV, measured thickness, and grain-size proportions only. Dynamic density used in the paper's inertial stress derives from paired mean pressure; neither it nor their processed conditional stresses are release-independent force inputs. Static density from physical preparation is a distinct control. Qualitative published dependence of agitation on grain size, weak volume dependence and fitted exponent range are known before any native workbook values. Existing corpus includes a distinct annular shear cell and an empty-water wave tank, with no reported prior exposure to these experiments.
