# Recorded-time prediction in repeated planar pushes

Source qualification only. No measured trajectories, force arrays, held terminal poses or exclusion-list records had been opened at the time of this specification. The required source is the 2017 Bauza–Rodriguez rect1 validation archive together with its matching list of unreliable recordings. The 2016 comprehensive archive and OmniPush cover different collections. The paper describes 100 repeated nominal 10 mm pushes at 20 mm/s, 31 starting angles and 11 starting positions on a square face. Multiplying those counts gives an expected design, not a verified census of eligible captures.

## Recorded times and permissible information

The author’s original converter c8352f5fe9a4f03aa07bf3753e04ca7898df6e1f preserves the force and object message header stamps. Its pusher stream uses either a robot log timestamp or an RRI message header, depending on the acquisition option. It flips the force x and torque z signs to express the measured reaction on the pusher in robot coordinates. The pose is from Vicon. The public prose calls its key obj_pose, while the converter and preprocessing code use object_pose. The actual archive must resolve this before any scoring. The converting code cannot establish which recording option generated all 2017 files.

The author-endorsed pdproc revision b187315443eb269c95d2b1d6ce9e1ef047facf1b averages intervals and interpolates missing bins independently across entire records. A gap crossing a forecast boundary could admit later observations. For the prospective prefix reader, preserve the recorded timestamp, truncate each ordered stream first, then sample by last observation carried forward. Only source stamps at or before each query are admissible. Identical duplicates collapse. Conflicting duplicates, unordered streams and nonfinite stamps cause a recorded refusal. Missing or stale values stay missing. Every role shares the same occurrence, timestamp and missingness policy. The input reader in prefix_clock.py includes a sentinel for a future observation whose value cannot be accessed. Source order is a trusted intake contract and must be verified from timestamps before feature extraction. No physical record is decoded by its synthetic checks.

Use zero offsets on the original recorded clocks. Do not fit lags from held signals, apply acausal interpolation, use zero-phase filters or align clips by endpoint agreement. The separate original-robot delay tests derive tip pose from latest-common TF and stamp with a joint-state header, while one optical fixture logs a marker rather than the tracked object stream. A measured delay from that pair does not establish the end-to-end latency of the 2017 archive. Zero offset defines a recorded-time analysis only. Event capture times and online availability would require independent matching calibration. Reject inconsistent epochs. The direction or magnitude of a delay must never be selected from the held prediction errors.

## Physical and statistical scope

Hold out complete nominal geometry cells with all their repetitions. Reserve side-0 initial positions 0.2 and 0.8 including all starting angles, keeping both halves of the square's mirror pairing together. No geometry parameters, outcome statistics, clock fits or lag choices from those cells enter construction. Preserve the source exclusion list before taking any source or held trace. Report absent files, malformed clocks and physical coverage separately for each reserved geometry. Repetitions within one apparatus or surface are not independent material experiments.

The proposed contrast is incremental prediction from early force and moment measured on the pusher after giving all competitors identical nominal geometry and early pusher/object poses. Compare the augmented candidate with the identical pose-only learner, a geometry-only distribution model, pose extrapolation and a deterministic shuffled-wrench control that preserves each entire prefix in a nominal geometry. That shuffle tests whether pairing an individual push with its own wrench matters. It cannot be used in an online single-trial forecast. The authors' released heteroscedastic GP or an explicitly labeled reconstruction is the historical comparator; its inputs and training support require separate qualification. No impersonation of an unavailable executable.

Once source identity is confirmed, a designated training geometry may be examined to qualify the available release marker, units and data rates. Only then fix all dates/IDs, release marker, local sample grid, maximum source age, missingness policy, endpoint definition, model classes, fit budget and aggregate/worst-case acceptance in a second prospective registration. No held geometry can be opened before that completion and the frozen forecast collection. Source training cannot use the reserved geometry or its mirrored records.

A tangential relative velocity computed across a documented active contact is kinematic evidence about sliding. Contact activation and separation depend on the probe footprint and geometry, and stick/slip labels are absent unless a separate source establishes them. Sensor wrench is distinct from the distributed support friction. Even perfect net force/moment information alone does not determine a unique distribution of base friction. A forecast improvement would establish useful information in the recorded prefix, not identification of microscopic contact conditions or a universal friction law.
