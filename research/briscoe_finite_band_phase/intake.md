# Source qualification for a rubidium polarizer readout

Briscoe et al. (2024), DOI 10.1088/1361-6455/ad5e24, deposit two measured datasets and six notebooks under DOI 10.15128/r2x920fw90c. The only proposed experimental target here is the **complete separate crossed-analyzer intensity acquisition in the longitudinal-field setup**. The circular and horizontal transmission scans at that setup would serve as inputs. The original authors already demonstrated indirect rotation from the two eigenmode transmissions and fitted their four outputs simultaneously. Neither the Hilbert method itself nor a historical four-channel fit is a prospectively held output. Check the notebook sources, measurement channel assignments, live-time averaging, reference-cell and etalon frequency calibration, single-scan offsets, physical intensity normalization, analyzer-angle calibration and the exact accessible ElecSus implementation before any measurement access.

Under a linear, homogeneous, nondepolarizing ideal sample in the Faraday geometry, the two circular field transfers have magnitudes sqrt(Tplus) and sqrt(Tminus). An ideal crossed linear analyzer transmits one quarter of their squared difference in complex field. Relative phase matters beyond the transmitted-amplitude envelope. An unknown downstream analyzer rotation and relative constant phase are confounded in the interference term. Finite detuning measurements bound the phase only with a declared out-of-band continuation and an optical field model. An outside-band difference in absorption changes in-band dispersion without any change in in-band measured absorption. Ordinary causality alone also permits all-pass factors unless the field transfer is minimum-phase with a fixed high-frequency limit. The directly accessible test is conditional output prediction, not unique window or vapor dispersion.

A distinctive candidate would model unmeasured spectral wings with the Rb Hamiltonian response fitted exclusively to allowed source scans, and use the measured circular difference to correct the model on the covered band. Compare the authentic published direct finite-window Hilbert processing, the fully model-based ElecSus forecast with the same calibration observations, zero-phase and fixed-tail alternatives. The measured crossed-analyzer record, its background, its frequency shift, and post-outcome joint fit must remain outside each predictor. If the registered release lacks independent frequency/photometric calibration needed for such a comparison, preserve the specific limit. Archive membership and software availability precede any numerical scans. This is an archival reanalysis; the published qualitative fits and the reported typical order of scan/analyzer offsets are known.

Do not transfer directly from the longitudinal to the transverse setup. The paper documents a roughly 98.2% 87Rb longitudinal cell and natural-abundance transverse cell, different atomic references and separate setups. The often useful low-susceptibility square-root product identity for equal isotopic mixtures and identical conditions cannot be tested directly as a geometry-only effect on these two specimens. No response values, joint-fit parameters or figures have been used here.
