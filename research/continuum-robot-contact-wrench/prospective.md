# Measured contact moments on the Toronto continuum robot

## Question, exposure and independent unit

For the single physical tendon-released *push_retract* recording, does distributing the published component masses over measured disk positions reduce bending-moment error compared with the actual pose-transformed contact wrench, a uniform rod of equal mass and strong calibration-only predictors? The observed result is a finite one-recording transfer. The analysis is offline. Biased axial force is not an estimate of absolute loading.

Fit only on all 16 quasi_static and all 10 dynamic_motion runs using measured shape and ATI target, plus contact_motion/touch_base for shared mounting calibration. Use no target values from the four subsequent contact paths. Primary path: push_retract, with released tendons and slow hand-imposed tip contact. Compulsory descriptive stress paths: plane_y_contact_midway (wand at the 18 cm disk), plane_y_poke_x and plane_y_poke_y (actuated transient perturbations). ## Frames, calibration, and numerical contracts

Use the released 100 Hz processed (15 Hz zero-phase prefiltered) source quantities. Their book-keeping clock is the intersection of acquisition streams. The original ATI acquisition applied its six-by-six calibration matrix after subtracting the mean of the first ten 1 kHz voltage samples, then averaged five samples per output. Preprocessing subtracts 2.5 ms from ATI block-return timestamps. Absolute untared force is unavailable. The Resense torque is divided by 1000 (mNm to Nm) during preprocessing. Use each file's matching source clock, full finite arrays, declared dimensions and recorded row order. Reject and report structural deficiencies before scoring. Retain rather than filter poor tracking where the processed values remain finite. This predicts offline filtered responses, with no claim of real-time latency.

The fixed source optical reference uses the mean location of the first 120 base poses, fixed rotation, and per-disk straight-configuration corrections. Processed mocap_frames.csv has five contiguous blocks in disk order, each [roll,pitch,yaw,x,y,z], in radians/metres. contact_pose.csv is [roll,pitch,yaw,x,y,z] of the actual sensor after the fixed Rx(pi/2)Rz(pi/6) and (0,-0.1137,0) m translation. Never apply that transformation twice. For the supplied intrinsic XYZ angles form R; r is the saved sensor origin. With component order [Fx,Fy,Fz,Mx,My,Mz], the exact source baseline before optional fixture correction is F=−R Fw and M=−R Mw+r×F. Preserve this unconstrained authored arm on its own. Positive z in the aligned robot frame runs from base to the hanging tip. The target is the tared ATI base moment in that same source frame. Let p_j(t) be the corrected positions of all five disks, s=j*0.12 m. Subtract the first 100 processed positions' average separately for each disk in each recording to approximate its pose at the earlier hardware tare. Never subtract any held ATI reading or select a clock alignment. Show the full time interval and raw outcome at the worst evaluated row.

Apply a fourth-order 2 Hz zero-phase Butterworth along time to the transported wrench and to each complete prediction and target using the released source's uniform-grid linear resampling and 3*(max(len(a),len(b))-1) edge padding on each side. Apply it once per term; a linear sum of prefiltered terms is equivalent. ## Frozen arms

1. Exact unadjusted author adjoint. Its June preprint documents forces, and its v3 source computes three force components under the contact mask and whole-record six-axis diagnostics. Keep the complete true/estimated six-vector.
2. Shared extra calibration from touch_base alone. Fit one constant origin displacement delta and constant moment intercept b by ordinary least squares on all rows of touch after both signals pass the comparison filter: M_ATI−M_source = delta×F_source+b. Fit to all three torque components jointly in SI units with numpy.linalg.lstsq rcond=1e−10. The fitted delta is an effective correction, not an independently measured physical offset. Record rank, singular values, fitted displacement and bias. Every arm below and the calibrated-adjoint-only arm use exactly this same correction once. No per-test offset, re-zeroing, rotation adjustment or recalibration.
3. Uniform-only geometry controls at 102.00 g (the actual GVS source's effective rod) and 101.27 g (the physical component sum). Approximate the arc-length average by trapezoidal integration over the five equally spaced measured disk positions. Add its change in first moment crossed with g=(0,0,9.81) m/s² to the calibrated source arm. These controlled comparisons use observed geometry; neither is presented as the full GVS numerical simulation.
4. Discrete physical candidate. Use the 5.90 g uniform backbone, 41.22 g base disk, seven 5.25 g intermediate disks, 8.90 g tip disk, and five 1.70 g marker sets at the five tracked disks. Approximate the four untracked disk positions by the average of their two tracked neighbors. Approximate each mass by its disk center; the exact assembled COM and marker configuration are unavailable. Compute the change in the total first moment from the fixed per-run optical reference. Take its cross product with g and add to the shared calibrated adjoint. Do not fit any masses or signs. A 102 g uniform model and a 101.27 g uniform model are separate controls. Gravity contributes zero to the z-moment in this declared frame, so z is an unchanged torsional diagnostic.
5. Same-information empirical controls trained on *free* motion before contact target access. Each predicts the tared noncontact bending-moment contribution from the five base-relative disk positions using the same 100-sample input reference. The calibrated measured-wand contribution is then added unchanged. Include a regularized linear map and nonlinear random Fourier feature ridge with an explicit linear skip, 96 cosine features, fixed seed 823120, fixed Gaussian-frequency scale 1/sqrt(number of features), fixed phase uniform[0,2pi). Inputs are all 15 position changes, their first and second centered temporal derivatives on the 2 Hz offline-filtered positional trace; first/last rows use one-sided differences. Train and standardize on every 4th row of each training recording for cost. Weight each training recording equally; all test rows retained. Fit a training-only intercept. Select ridge lambda independently per arm from {1e−6,1e−4,1e−2,1,100} by minimum mean whole-record squared vector bending error across four fixed folds: alphabetically sorted 26 training path IDs at indices 0,4,... belong to fold0; 1,5,... fold1; etc. If equal choose the larger penalty. Recompute centering and scaling in each fold, replace standard deviations <1e−8 by 1. Fit final parameters on all 26, freeze models, all four forecasts and exact clocks before loading any held ATI target. Noncontact targets may contain actuator/dynamic contributions. This transfer baseline is an empirical prediction, not a fitted plant mass model.

The v3 GVS main simulates noncontact differential tendon tensions, gravity, six bending coordinates and zero full tip wrench, from a straight initial state. Its unchanged output cannot use the measured external wand load. Its published constant 102 g has been retained as the explicit uniform-mass comparator; fitting or adding a contact force changes that GVS model. Report this version distinction and do not label a reimplementation as the original GVS solution.

## Decision and receiving

Measure whole-path planar vector RMSE, sqrt(mean[(Mxhat−Mx)^2+(Myhat−My)^2]), and maximum instantaneous planar Euclidean error in Nm. The primary admission gate requires strictly lower whole push_retract vector RMSE than *each* of the two adjoint, two uniform and two training-only empirical controls. A separate worst-case gate requires strictly lower maximum planar error than all six. Joint = both. Also report contact-mask RMSE and peak against all controls, individual moment RMSEs (including unchanged torsion in the physical arms), all three stress paths, and author force axes. Neither contact-only nor actuated results can rescue a failed whole-path gate. Capture raw timestamps, predicted/actual moment/force and per-arm increments at the respective worst instant, with its pose history. Independent receiving reopens raw original processed matrices, checks source and forecast SHA, checks all clocks and recounts every unrounded score/worst. No refitting or changing phases after the fixed outcome access.
