Interpret the experiment
  • The baseline is linear in angle. Each alternative adds one continuous hinge: max(0, θ − κ). Slopes and nuisance coefficients are fitted together.
  • The ranking uses a caller-assumed noise floor and penalizes fitted coefficients plus the searched change angle. This is a heuristic, without calibrated probabilities or confidence intervals.
  • A kink can reflect omitted smooth response, instrument effects or other confounding. It does not identify contact, mechanism topology, physical stiffness or causation by itself.
  • Positive slopes are allowed. Nothing here establishes passivity, physical safety, valid extrapolation or permission to actuate hardware.
  • The input notebook preserves supplied measurements and assumptions. Opening it recomputes results. This page neither uploads observations nor saves them automatically.
